Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do tennis lessons cost in Clermont, FL?

Pricing varies based on lesson type and duration. Contact Coach Tommy directly at (407) 259-0774 or through the contact form for current rates on private, semi-private, and group lessons. Package discounts are available.

What age can kids start tennis lessons?

Coach Tommy works with junior players starting from age 4 and up, using age-appropriate techniques, scaled equipment, and drills that make learning fun from the very first lesson.

Do I need my own racquet to start?

Not necessarily. Reach out before your first lesson and Coach Tommy can advise on the right racquet size and equipment based on age and skill level — so you don't buy the wrong thing.

Where does Coach Tommy teach lessons?

Coach Tommy travels to courts throughout Clermont, Winter Garden, Windermere, Davenport, Minneola, Horizon West, and the greater Central Florida area. He comes to your community — no long drive required.

Is it too late to learn tennis as an adult?

Not at all. Some of Coach Tommy's most rewarding students are adults picking up the game for the first time at 40, 50, or beyond. Tennis is a lifelong sport and there's no wrong age to start.

Do you offer lessons for competitive or tournament players?

Yes. Coach Tommy offers match strategy and competition prep sessions specifically for players preparing for USTA tournaments, high school tennis, or looking to sharpen their competitive game.

Ready to get on the court?
No pressure, no long-term commitment.
