Tennis in Windermere

Lessons that fit Windermere

Windermere is a tennis town. Between the town courts, the club courts, and the HOA courts, there are a lot of people here who want to play.

But here's the thing most people don't know. The Town of Windermere's six public courts are for residents of the incorporated town only, with an annual court key. And the town doesn't let coaches teach non-residents there.

A lot of people with a Windermere mailing address actually live in unincorporated Orange County, so they don't qualify. That's fine. Tommy will meet you at your neighborhood or HOA court, or at one of his regular spots nearby.

Questions

Tennis lessons in Windermere: common questions

Can Coach Tommy teach me on the Windermere town courts?

Only if you're a resident of the incorporated Town of Windermere with a current court key. Town rules allow a member to use an outside coach for individual lessons, first come, first served. Coaches can't teach non-residents there.

I have a Windermere address. Am I a town resident?

Maybe not. Many Windermere mailing addresses are in unincorporated Orange County. The Town of Windermere at (407) 876-2563 can tell you for sure.

Will Tommy come to my HOA or club court?

Yes, as long as your community allows outside coaches. A lot of them do, but it's worth checking first.
