Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Serving Aces Tennis Academy ("we," "us," or "Coach Tommy") operates the website servingaces.net. This page explains what information we collect when you visit our site and how we use it.

What information we collect

Contact form submissions

When you fill out the lesson inquiry form on our Contact page, we collect the name, email address, phone number, and message you provide. We use that information solely to respond to your inquiry and help you book a lesson. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your information with third parties.

Cookies and analytics

Our site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors find and use the site — for example, which pages are most visited and what search terms brought people here. This data is aggregated and anonymous; it does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

No user accounts

Our site does not offer user registration or accounts. We do not store login credentials for visitors.

No payment processing on this site

We do not collect or store payment information. If you pay for lessons, that is handled directly in person or through a separate payment service.

How we use your information

The only personal information we receive is what you voluntarily submit through our contact form. We use it to:

  • Respond to your lesson inquiry
  • Schedule and confirm lesson appointments
  • Follow up if you asked a specific question

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for as long as needed to manage your inquiry or ongoing lesson schedule, and then deleted. If you would like us to delete any information you have sent us, email us at tommy@servingaces.net and we will do so promptly.

Third-party services

Our site uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Formspree — contact form processing (Formspree Privacy Policy)

Your rights

You have the right to request a copy of any personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make any of these requests, contact us at:

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
